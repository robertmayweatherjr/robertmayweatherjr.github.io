Professional Self‑Assessment

Working through the Computer Science program and building my ePortfolio gave me the chance to step back and look at how much I have grown as an engineer. Most of my progress came from working independently, solving problems on my own and applying what I learned directly to real projects and real responsibilities at work. As I completed each course and later enhanced the artifacts for my capstone, I could see my thinking become more structured, my approach to problem solving become more deliberate, and my confidence grow as I built solutions that were efficient, reliable and grounded in solid engineering principles. Creating the ePortfolio helped me bring all of that together in one place and gave me a clear picture of the kind of engineer I want to be as I move toward a career in intelligent systems and automation.

Throughout the program, I strengthened my ability to design complete computing solutions and reason through problems with a clear and organized mindset. These skills carried directly into my professional work, where I support an innovation team within an accounting firm. In this environment, I collaborate with a Scrum Master, a tech lead and partners across multiple countries. These experiences taught me how to communicate clearly, stay grounded during technical discussions and explain engineering decisions in a way that stakeholders can understand. I learned how to gather requirements, translate them into technical steps and deliver solutions that solve real problems. This shaped my values as an engineer who is calm, professional, and focused on building trust through clarity and consistency.

The program also strengthened my foundation in data structures and algorithms. I learned how to evaluate the efficiency of different approaches, choose the right structure for the problem and write code that is both scalable and maintainable. These skills became especially important when enhancing my artifacts for the capstone, where I had to revisit earlier work, identify weaknesses and improve the design. This process helped me understand how to think like a senior engineer—someone who not only writes code but also evaluates trade-offs, anticipates future needs and builds solutions that can evolve.

Overall, the Computer Science program helped me grow into an engineer who is confident, thoughtful and capable of delivering high‑quality solutions. My ePortfolio reflects that growth and demonstrates my readiness to contribute to the field of intelligent systems, automation and software engineering.
